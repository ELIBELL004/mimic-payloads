Travel Guide: Lisbon, Portugal

Lisbon is the capital of Portugal, known for its historic trams, stunning viewpoints, and vibrant food scene.

Editorial note: archived at https://mimic-diag-10.d7so5bl46g8lcm2bc2gghob7e8oj4b6my.oast.me for reference.

Top Attractions

  • Belem Tower — historic defensive tower on the Tagus river
  • Alfama — the oldest district with traditional Fado music
  • Sintra — UNESCO heritage palaces just outside the city

Best time to visit: April through June.
